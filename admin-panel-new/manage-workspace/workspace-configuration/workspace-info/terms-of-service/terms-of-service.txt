1.
Title: Should workspace terms of service remain disabled by default

Pre-condition:
New workspace should be available

Steps:
# Login to the new workspace as Workspace Admin
# Go to Manage Workspace > Workspace Configuration > Workspace Info page
# Click on the "Terms of Service" tab

Expectation:
Workspace terms of service remains disabled

2.
Title: Should enable workspace terms of service

Steps:
# Login to the workspace as Workspace Admin
# Go to Manage Workspace > Workspace Configuration > Workspace Info page
# Click on the "Terms of Service" tab
# Check the "Terms of Service" checkbox
# Add the terms of service
# Click on the Save button

Expectation:
Workspace terms of service acceptance page will be shown

3.
Title: Should update workspace terms of service notifying the users about the updates

Steps:
# Login to the workspace as Workspace Admin
# Go to Manage Workspace > Workspace Configuration > Workspace Info page
# Click on the "Terms of Service" tab
# Check the "Terms of Service" checkbox
# Add the terms of service
# Check the "Notify users about the updates" checkbox
# Click on the "Save" button

Expectation:
Workspace terms of service will be updated
Worksapce terms of service acceptance page will be shown

4.
Title: Should update workspace terms of service without notifying the users about the updates

Steps:
# Login to the workspace as Workspace Admin
# Go to Manage Workspace > Workspace Configuration > Workspace Info page
# Click on the "Terms of Service" tab
# Check the "Terms of Service" checkbox
# Add the terms of service
# Uncheck the "Notify users about the updates" checkbox
# Click on the "Save" button

Expectation:
Workspace terms of service will be updated
Worksapce terms of service acceptance page will not be shown

5.
Title: Should cancel updating workspace terms of service

Steps:
# Login to the workspace as Workspace Admin
# Go to Manage Workspace > Workspace Configuration > Workspace Info page
# Click on the "Terms of Service" tab
# Update the terms of service
# Click on the Cancel button

Expectation:
Workspace terms of service update will be cancelled
